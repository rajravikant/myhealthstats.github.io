core exercises for stability

Core exercises for stability are essential for improving posture and preventing injuries. Key exercises include planks, bridges, Russian twists, bird dogs, and dead bugs. These exercises strengthen core muscles, enhancing stability and supporting overall spine health and posture.

Spot fat reduction possible?

Spot fat reduction is not possible through targeted exercises or treatments. Fat loss occurs gradually across the entire body with a combination of proper nutrition, regular exercise, and a healthy lifestyle. There's no way to selectively reduce fat in specific areas.

Dynamic stretching vs static stretching

Dynamic stretching involves active movements that mimic the activity you're about to perform, warming up muscles and increasing range of motion. Static stretching involves holding a stretch for a prolonged period to improve flexibility. Dynamic stretching is ideal before exercise, while static stretching is better for post-workout cool-down and flexibility improvement.
